## How it works

1. **[Download](#download) and install** `TwitchChat-Setup.exe`.
2. **Enter** a channel name in **General** and click **Apply channel**.
3. **Sign in** to Twitch. Your chat opens.

<p align="center">
  <img src="images/step-general.png" alt="General settings with a channel name and Sign in to Twitch" width="100%"><br>
  <sub>Enter a channel and sign in.</sub>
</p>

4. **Style** it: click a layer to change Background, Text, Emotes or Window.

<p align="center">
  <img src="images/step-background.png" alt="Background layer with opacity set to 50%" width="100%"><br>
  <sub>Turn the background down to see your game.</sub>
</p>

## Settings

Changes save automatically.

| Section | What you change |
| --- | --- |
| **General** | Twitch account, channel, chat view, updates |
| **Window** | Always on top, frame, lock, Click-Through, size and position |
| **Background** | Color, opacity, saved color pairs, **Pick from desktop** |
| **Text** | Font, message, input and status font size, outline, spacing |
| **Emotes** | Emote providers, animations, GIFs, badges, celebrations |
| **Twitch extras** | Hide leaderboard, Hype Train, pinned messages or the Cheers button |

## Shortcuts

| Keys | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| <kbd>Tab</kbd> / <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> | Complete a username or emote, next or previous match |
| <kbd>↑</kbd> / <kbd>↓</kbd> | Bring back messages you sent in this chat |
| <kbd>Alt</kbd> + drag | Move the window while the frame is hidden |

## Privacy

Your Twitch sign-in **stays on your PC**, in the app's own session. **Sign out** clears it.
The app connects to Twitch, to 7TV, BetterTTV and FrankerFaceZ for their emotes, and checks for updates. You can turn each emote service off in **Emotes**. No ads.

## Uninstall

1. **Quit** the app from the tray.
2. **Uninstall** **Twitch Chat** in Windows Settings.
3. **Tick** **Also delete settings, Twitch sign-in and cached data** to remove everything the app saved on your PC.

Leave it unticked to keep your settings and sign-in for a later reinstall.
